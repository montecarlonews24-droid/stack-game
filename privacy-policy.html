Privacy Policy

STACK! Tower Rush
Last updated: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MonteCarlo News24 ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the STACK! Tower Rush mobile game ("the Game").

1. Information We Collect

The Game may collect the following information:

  • Player name and score: Stored on our Firebase Realtime Database for the global leaderboard.
  • Device information: Basic device data collected automatically by third-party services.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Display scores on the global leaderboard.
  • Improve gameplay experience.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Firebase – for leaderboard data storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – for displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

Leaderboard data (player name and score) is stored on Firebase servers located in the United States. We do not store any personally identifiable information beyond what you voluntarily enter as your player name.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your leaderboard data, contact us at: montecarlo.news24@gmail.com

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
